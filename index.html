About me

I am an Electrical Engineer who works across MEP design, site installation and high-voltage testing.

From the test bench to the construction site.

I am an Electrical and Electronics Engineer (Power and Control) from Kathmandu University. My career started in high-voltage testing and commissioning, where I tested transformers, circuit breakers and switchgear up to 132 kV, following IEC and IEEE safety protocols.

Today I work as an Electrical Engineer on MEP projects in Kathmandu. I supervise installation, review shop drawings, material submittals and method statements, and coordinate with architects, structural engineers, contractors and consultants. Before that I designed electrical installations for commercial and industrial projects and prepared AutoCAD drawings and specifications.
